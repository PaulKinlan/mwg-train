Paul decides whether to spend **after** the evidence and a dated cost range are put in front of him. This is the planning parent of the cost-per-accepted-pair task `mwg-train-f92`, not its price sheet. ## Gate 1 — sufficient independent, eligible data

**Proposed full first-run bar for owner review:** at least **200 accepted, full-flow generation pairs** from at least **42 independent generation families**: two distinct topics/role-and-state scenarios for **each of the 21 behavioural archetypes** across the five groups in [`EXPANSION-PLAN.md`](briefs/EXPANSION-PLAN.md). Cover all seven supported framework arms with at least ten accepted pairs each and at least two genuinely working frameworks per qualifying family. These are *minimum coverage floors*, not a full Cartesian product, a promise of yield, or a reason to add a fake framework. The ~200-pair initial scale comes from the experiment's planning target in [`docs/design-brief-2026-10-08.md`](../design-brief-2026-10-08.md), not a power calculation; decide the final target against observed usable-data yield, family diversity, exact tokenizer volume and Paul's budget **before** freezing. If a framework cannot implement a family's journey, record the cell as missing rather than duplicate a generic form. Do not count two voices, seven nearly identical framework shells, or seven screenshots as independent families.

A qualifying family has a distinct scenario/content fixture, independent prompt and design target, primary and secondary role goals (or an explicit rationale for a single-role task), applicable rule IDs, all observable journeys including empty/failure/recovery, and a cluster ID that groups variants and near-duplicate topics, templates, scaffolds, design assets and seeds. Report a coverage matrix by **archetype × role × state × rule × framework** with attempted, accepted, rejected and unsupported counts; publish the missing cells and reject reasons. Do not use a blanket yield target to turn bad pairs into accepted pairs. Keep **generation** and **repair** sets separate: a repair adapter needs its own approved defect originals, fixed-defect oracles, volume and secondary endpoint before it can justify *its own* paid run. Five current repair families do not make the generation set larger.

Only an immutable, uniquely identified original → uplift record with retained source trees, pinned rule snapshot, exact edit/diff, original and uplifted browser artifacts, provenance graph and independent acceptance verdict counts as an eligible pair. Phrasing variants are prompts for one family, not new training examples unless their serialization and effect are explicitly specified; no model-generated implementations exist in this repository yet. `A1_self_generated` originals and `A2_mwg_uplift_deterministic` outputs require explicit per-row training approval and a clear ancestor chain. `A3` teacher, `A4`/`A5` reproduction and `A6` evaluation assets stay excluded under [the rights policy](../provenance/README.md); self-authored layout boards remain excluded. Scan **both sides and the record** for owner identity/credentials. No real payments, personal data, production authentication secrets, or unlicensed images/copy.

**Gate evidence:** a versioned candidate/accepted/rejected manifest with family and near-duplicate clusters, tasks, framework, source/target hashes, rights approval and exclusion reason; reproducible counts and a reviewer-signed coverage table. **NO-GO** if the minimum is filled with correlated variants, unsupported workflows, unapproved provenance, or overlapping evaluation clusters.

## Gate 2 — a pair-quality bar that cannot be passed by a screenshot

Every *included* pair must independently pass the following, with artifacts attached to its hashes; reject the pair if evidence is missing or inconclusive. Fixing the harness is not permission to waive a brief's requirement.

1. **Complete contract and real behaviour.** The original and uplifted trees install/build/start with pinned tools; document any *intended* defect of an original repair example. On the uplifted tree, every declared route is served with its declared method; every role-specific journey, assertion, server-owned state transition, reload, validation failure, empty/error recovery and permission boundary promised by the brief is driven in a real browser. Drive the corresponding original baseline to establish the actual before-state and preserve its non-defective task content and state through uplift. A 200 response, `spec.json`, one submit/echo or a generated HTML file does not satisfy this gate. Record the prior defect and proven improvement for repair, or the pre/post applicable property for generation; an already-modern original with no warranted fix is a control, **not** a manufactured positive pair.
2. **Intentional visual quality.** The per-family design contract states a visual thesis, information hierarchy, content/asset provenance, tokens, responsive layout and meaningful loading/empty/error/success states. Capture desktop and 390px mobile **before/after** images tied to the running routes; inspect controls at keyboard focus and 200% text zoom, no page overflow/clipped actions. Independently reject fixture-grade repeated form scaffolds, placeholder or lorem text, indistinguishable archetypes, unreadable typography, a static card posing as an interactive product, or imagery presented without rights or purpose. Evaluate suitability to the *specific* task, not similarity to a concept board or a single universal visual template. Log reviewer judgement and adjudicate disagreement; a screenshot without functional/browser proof is not acceptance.
3. **Pinned MWG applicability and no regressions.** Test the brief's predeclared `required_rules` against the pinned [`docs/eval/rules.json`](../eval/rules.json) with rule-specific observable checks; report rule-ID denominators and applicability. Require a verified improvement where the pair claims one, not merely an API name or a cosmetic CSS diff; reject over-application and broken previously-modern behaviour. Label this repository's deterministic baseline as **mwg-train deterministic baseline**, never as an official `web-uplift` output. Use [`docs/eval/ATTRIBUTION.md`](../eval/ATTRIBUTION.md) for the distinction.
4. **Accessibility and security blockers: zero in accepted pairs.** Test accessible labels/names, keyboard reachability and focus, field-associated/announced errors and applicable contrast/responsive semantics. For relevant auth/state flows, check session-cookie attributes, authorisation, sanitised HTML, CSRF protection and no client/record secrets. Automated scanners inform review but never replace browser interaction, keyboard inspection or focused exploit checks. The owner-auth scan must pass fail-closed on both trees and their records under the versioned identity configuration; synthetic demo users remain allowed when a brief requires auth.

**Gate evidence:** per-pair full-brief route/journey matrix, browser traces and before/after screenshots, build logs, MWG-rule verdicts, a11y/security and owner-auth reports, original/uplift hashes, human review and explicit rejection categories. Re-run the real journeys after any repair, not just the formerly failing check. **NO-GO** while the shared builder cannot serve routes/flows that the proposed corpus claims, or while a visual target is being treated as actual generated output.

## Gate 3 — valid, held-out evaluation before the final test is touched

Preserve the existing [preregistration](../eval/PREREGISTRATION.md) and its current v2 seal (`sha256:89a1f47d638c5eab733074d87bc19d6615401aeb9a77ecbd86cf10b443e272b1`): 79 briefs/44 families, with **12 dev** and **32 sealed test** families; the test contains ten in-family, twenty out-of-family and two repair families. The seal hash is recorded today, but the document says the formal freeze happens at the start of the first run; do not silently claim an earlier irrevocable freeze. Verify the seal and the pinned guidance/check implementation together. Do **not** mutate sealed briefs or use their targets, prompts, assets, or test outcomes for corpus generation or tuning. Use the dev set for rubric/tool debugging and stop before touching test.

Enforce whole-family and near-duplicate-cluster disjointness at prompt, topic, code/scaffold, screenshot/design and generator-seed levels; the current family-ID/target-hash gate is necessary but not sufficient for those semantic near-duplicates. Keep sibling variants and shared templates in one split. Have a reviewer inspect flagged cluster neighbors with recorded reasons, not just an automated similarity score. For newly authored archetypes **not represented by the current sealed test**, independently design and preregister a *new*, clean out-of-family holdout **before** building/training on those archetypes; do not quietly expand the old sealed endpoint, borrow a sibling prompt, or declare the old study powered to decide the new categories. Changes to preregistered endpoints/splits require a dated deviation or a separate study and explicit owner review.

The existing primary endpoint remains **all five together**: runnable, every journey/assertion, all required MWG rules, no blocker accessibility and no blocker security findings. When a run is separately approved, compare T with C1 bare base, C2 same base plus pinned applicable guidance, and C3 same base plus deterministic uplift under equal prompt/decoding/retry/token budget and one pinned evaluator; repair is a distinct secondary endpoint. Report per-family/archetype/rule applicability, already-modern regressions, cost/time, and family-cluster-bootstrap confidence intervals with the preregistered H1/H2/H3 decision rules. Blinded two-rater human assessment covers at least 20% of sealed test briefs with adjudication. A single prettier image, training loss, self-reported guide names, or a dev result cannot be reported as sealed-test success. Execute the final test once, after every tunable choice is frozen; preserve failures and negative outcomes.

**Gate evidence:** seal/check/tool hashes, split-and-neighbor audit, fixed arm/scaffold/judge/decoding records, executable browser oracle for every sealed task, precommitted analysis plan and an independent evaluation dry audit using **dev-only** fixtures. **NO-GO** if test data influenced the training set, the new taxonomy lacks its own uncontaminated assessment, or full-brief functional evaluation is not executable.

## Gate 4 — explicit decision before a billable action

The training seam and costs must become executable evidence, not a promise: validate approved and disjoint manifest bytes/hash/rows, independently re-count the *actual serialized* prompt + code targets with the **chosen checkpoint's tokenizer**, test max-context/truncation, pin base revision/licence and a reproducible recipe, and demonstrate the candidate backend can train and serve the exact pinned base. Backend parity is a comparison precondition, not grounds to pick one now. No backend or model is selected by this epic.

The coordinator-owned `mwg-train-f92` cost task must put its **updated number on its bead** for Paul: cost per *eligible accepted* pair for each comparable backend, with attempted/accepted counts, training tokens × epochs, pinned dated price/quote digests, standing adapter deployment/evaluation GPU time, storage and uncertainty. Price the smallest **billable** informative slice separately and state a bounded maximum charge and stop rule. The old projection using 210 accepted templates is not a spend decision. Any billable dry run or online inference is spend. The hub, not this project lane, asks Paul; record Paul's **verbatim, amount-scoped authorisation on the bead before executing anything** and record actual cost after. If rate, checkpoint reachability, accepted denominator or serving duration is unknown, the estimate is `UNVERIFIED`, not $0. The calculation method is in [`docs/train/README.md`](README.md); the deferred `mwg-train-f92` owns fresh inputs and figures, not this planning page.

**Decision sequence:** quality and split reviewer verdicts → frozen dataset/recipe/evaluator evidence → updated f92 per-backend cost and cap → Paul explicitly approves *a specific priced slice* or declines → only then schedule that run and its sealed evaluation. If any earlier gate fails, remain **NO-GO** regardless of a cheap price. Passing these gates makes a request to Paul supportable, **never automatic spend authorisation**.
