# Provenance: editorial blog archetype visual reference boards

- **Scope:** four illustrative JPEGs for `editorial-blog`, an archetype authored on the archetype type axis (bead `mwg-train-efg`) rather than one of the five evaluation families. It is the first archetype added outside that sealed set, and this record exists so nobody has to guess which side of that boundary it sits on: **A1, planning reference only** - not an evaluation target, not a training example, not generated-site evidence.
- **Generation:** 2026-10-11 UTC, by `scripts/generate-archetype-boards.mjs --write --only editorial-blog`. The script passes `model: gemini-nano-banana-2.1` explicitly to the project's model gateway (`https://gemini.int.exe.xyz/v1beta/models/gemini-nano-banana-2.1:generateContent`, overridable with `MWG_TRAIN_IMAGE_ENDPOINT`) and fails closed unless the response's own `modelVersion` equals the requested model, so all four boards are gateway-attested `gemini-nano-banana-2.1`. Recorded settings: `responseModalities: ["IMAGE"]`, temperature 0.7, `maxOutputTokens` 8192; each board returned a 1376 × 768 JPEG. No seed was set, so the bytes are not reproducible from the prompts. The prompts are the four in the linked archetype README, and their exact UTF-8 digests are in the table below, so a regeneration can be proven to have used the same brief.
- **Rights:** [Google consumer-account terms](../accounts/google-antigravity-consumer.md) prohibit using AI-generated service content to develop machine-learning models. `excluded_from_training: true`; `approved_for_training: false` for **all four**. No exclusive copyright, general image license, production or commercial reuse clearance is claimed, and no separate reuse/clearance document was supplied. Do not move these images into a corpus, an evaluation target, adapter or training work, or generated-site evidence. A visual review does not change that boundary.
- **Visual truth:** the publication name, headlines, bylines, dates, article text, tag names and share counts are invented, unverified placeholders. The JPEGs do not demonstrate a working index, search, deep link, share path, composer or accessible reading experience, and they do not describe the archetype's contract: the pictures are broader than any behaviour that has been implemented.
- **Observed placeholder artefacts:** not yet inspected by an independent vision review. A review was commissioned on 2026-10-11 from a vision-capable model in a different family than the lane that produced these boards (the producing lane is text-only), and its findings - corrupted text in primary positions, cross-board inconsistency in placeholder content, and any state the prompt asked for and the board did not depict - are to be recorded here before these boards are used for anything beyond planning. Until that line is written, treat this set as GENERATED AND UNREVIEWED.
- **Coverage:** four boards, chosen so a journey can be written against a reader and an author without inventing a fifth state - the index, an article reached by deep link, the author's composer, and the empty-search state. Error and offline states are **not** pictured and must not be inferred from these images.
