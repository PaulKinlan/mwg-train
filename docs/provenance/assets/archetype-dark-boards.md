# Dark-mode archetype boards: byte inventory, not model attestation

The [per-board register](archetype-dark-boards.json) records all **61** committed JPEGs under `docs/design/archetypes/*/dark/`: the path, SHA-256 of current bytes, byte length, measured JPEG-header dimensions, source-evidence status, and explicit model/prompt/rights/visual-review status. `test/archetype-dark-provenance.test.mjs` checks every entry against the tree, requires the complete 61-path inventory, and refuses silently changed bytes or missing records. These boards are illustrative images, not functional demonstrations or evaluation targets.

- **54 generator-reported boards:** The historical [`generate-archetype-light-dark.mjs`](../../../scripts/generate-archetype-light-dark.mjs) requests `gemini-nano-banana-2.1` and rejects a response whose `modelVersion` differs. It wrote the JPEGs but did **not** persist each response, its exact composed prompt or a digest binding that response to the committed board. The committed script is evidence of its intended verification, **not** durable per-board model/prompt attestation. Accordingly, the register records `response_model: null`, `generation_prompt_sha256: null`, and `source_status: generator_reported_no_durable_receipt`. Do not infer exact generator inputs from today's README, `design.md`, or current Light images: those sources can change after generation.
- **Three copied Dark anchors:** For fan-community-site, realtime-telemetry-dashboard and scroll-storytelling-launch, `anchorMode: dark-anchor` copied the existing Light Step 1 into `dark/` before regenerating the Light version. Their Dark bytes match the previous tree at `52881d58` exactly, as recorded by `source_board_sha256` and `source_commit`; they were **not** returned by a new model call during the Light/Dark run. The prior Light asset's model/prompt receipts are not durably bound here, so these rows also retain null response/prompt fields.
- **Four browser-puzzle-game Dark imports:** Their files were copied from scratch paths during `15409c2b8`. No model response or generator-prompt receipt was retained. Their source status is `unverified_scratch_import`; see the [local Signal Grid provenance](../../design/archetypes/browser-puzzle-game/PROVENANCE.md) for the original Light Step 1 and three replaced Light boards. This register does **not** overwrite that history or convert a configured model name into an attested response.
- **Rights and vision boundary for all 61:** `excluded_from_training: true` and `approved_for_training: false` are project **policy**, not proof that reuse is legally forbidden or permitted. `reuse_clearance: null` means provider terms and product/training reuse clearance are not established per board. `vision_verdict: null` means a [frame-scan SHA pin](../../../.fleet/frame-scan-pins.json) cannot substitute for a recorded per-image vision review. Do not feed these assets to training or claim playable UI, frame-wide visual acceptance, commercial reuse rights, or sealed A6 provenance from this inventory.

A future change may replace null fields **only with authentic per-board receipts** binding the returned model identity, exact input prompt (including image references), byte digest, rights source and independent review; otherwise leave them unknown. Re-generating images is a separately approved paid operation requiring an exact tariff and fail-closed reservation. Nothing here triggers a model call or changes image bytes.
